# Data Quality Pillar: Consistency

## 1. Definition

Consistency refers to whether data is represented in a uniform and compatible way across records, fields, datasets, or systems where the same meaning is expected.

Consistent data follows the expected representation and formatting rules.

---

## 2. Why Consistency Matters

Inconsistent data can cause:

- incorrect grouping;
- failed matching;
- duplicate categories;
- incorrect filtering;
- unreliable joins;
- transformation problems;
- reporting inconsistencies.

Even when individual values are technically valid, inconsistent representation can make the dataset difficult to use reliably.

---

## 3. Common Consistency Issues

Examples include:

- inconsistent capitalization;
- leading or trailing spaces;
- inconsistent text representation;
- inconsistent date formats;
- inconsistent units;
- inconsistent naming;
- inconsistent formatting;
- header/data mismatches.

The exact checks depend on the dataset and defined validation rules.
